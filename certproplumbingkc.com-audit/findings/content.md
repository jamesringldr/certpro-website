## E-E-A-T

**Experience (weak):** “We’ve completed thousands of plumbing jobs” is on a company introduced May 2026. Off-site bios show 30+ years at Zoom Drain / AB May. On-site, that career history is not attributed to named people, so the claim reads as the LLC’s track record.

**Expertise (weak):** No license number, no “Master Plumber” name on-page (LinkedIn has it). No city-specific diagnostic detail on service templates.

**Authoritativeness (emerging):** LinkedIn company + owner posts (May 2026), Liberty Chamber listing, Nextdoor mention. Almost no web citations of `certproplumbingkc.com`.

**Trust (weak):** HTTPS yes. No privacy policy, no terms, no physical address, no hours, no reviews/testimonials on-page, newsletter control is a non-submitting button, social icons are `href="#"`. Email is `info@certproplumbing.com` while the marketing domain is `certproplumbingkc.com`.

## Other content issues

- `/services` visible intro: “SEO-friendly service pages with reusable conversion sections…” — internal product copy shipped to production.
- Service pages render eyebrow **“Service Template”**.
- Homepage “Latest Plumbing Insights” cards have titles/dates/images but **Read More is a `<button>` with no href**. “View All Articles” points at `/services`. Dates are Jan 2026, before the May 2026 brand launch.
- Footer “Testimonials” links to `/contact`.
- Keyword stuffing of the phrase “fast rapid response” across chrome, hero, trust bar, and body.

## Recommendations

1. Rewrite service pages to 800+ words of unique, local, first-hand coverage (symptoms, KC-metro causes, what happens on a call, materials, when to replace vs repair). Do not clone one template paragraph.
2. Put Derek Drager (master plumber) and Ashley Drager on `/about` with credentials, years, and where prior “thousands of jobs” were earned. Attribute 30+ years to the plumber, not the 3-month LLC.
3. Remove or ship real insight URLs. Do not leave decoy article cards.
4. Add Privacy Policy and Terms. Wire footer links. Remove `href="#"`.
5. Replace `/services` intro and the “Service Template” eyebrow.
