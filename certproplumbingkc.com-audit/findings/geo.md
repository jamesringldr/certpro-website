Google’s position (Search Central): optimizing for AI search is still SEO. `llms.txt` is not a confirmed ranking lever. Findings below are citability and entity-clarity issues, not a separate “GEO hack” list.

## Citability

Homepage lead is usable but generic. Strongest extractable facts:

- Family-owned
- Phone +1 816-454-0247
- Licensed / bonded / insured
- Counties in MO/KS within ~50 miles of Jackson County
- “Real people answer”

Missing citable specifics: license number, named master plumber, hours, street or SAB statement, typical arrival window, review count, unique job stories.

Service pages are too short to be quoted (~80–110 unique words). Answers are not self-contained 134–167 word passages.

Front-load problem: the first 30% of the homepage is chrome + slogan, not a definitional “CertPro Plumbing is a Liberty/North KC family-owned plumber that…” block.

## Crawler access

No robots.txt, so GPTBot / PerplexityBot / Googlebot are not blocked. That is fine for citation. Dual hosts still confuse which URL to cite.

`/llms.txt` 404. Optional; do not treat as Critical. If added, keep it a short factual dump (NAP, services, counties, phone) after host lock.

## Recommendations

1. One host, one NAP, `sameAs` graph (LinkedIn, Chamber, GBP, Nextdoor).
2. Add a 2–3 sentence entity blurb at the top of Home and About with who / where / phone / license.
3. Put named expert + first-hand process on About and 2–3 flagship services (emergency, sewer, water heater).
4. Update Chamber (and any other citation) from `certproplumbing.com` / HTTP to the canonical HTTPS host.
