## Core Page Outlines

### `/` (Home)
**Primary intent:** Convert high-intent visitors to call/form action quickly.

**Trust + local requirements:**
- Family-owned and licensed messaging above first major scroll break.
- 24/7 rapid-response framing.
- Service area reference to North Kansas City metro + key cities.

**Copy outline:**
1. **Hero**
   - Headline: immediate help + local trust.
   - Subcopy: fast dispatch, professional care, clear next step.
   - CTA pair: primary call action + estimate fallback.
2. **Trust strip**
   - Family-owned, licensed/insured, 24/7 availability.
3. **Services preview**
   - 12-service overview with benefit-first summaries.
4. **Why homeowners choose CertPro**
   - Proof-oriented bullets: responsiveness, professionalism, transparent communication.
5. **Process in 3 steps**
   - Contact -> Diagnose -> Fix.
6. **Local service coverage**
   - Explicit city cluster reference.
7. **FAQ block**
   - Objections: response time, areas served, estimates, emergency availability.
8. **Final CTA band**
   - Urgent but calm close.

**FAQ block (schema-ready draft):**
- Q: "Do you offer 24/7 emergency plumbing in North Kansas City?"
  - A: "Yes. CertPro provides around-the-clock emergency plumbing support for homeowners in North Kansas City and nearby communities. If you call with an urgent issue, we prioritize rapid dispatch and communicate expected arrival timing as soon as possible."
- Q: "Which areas do you serve?"
  - A: "We serve North Kansas City and surrounding metro communities including Liberty, Gladstone, Parkville, Smithville, Platte City, Kearney, and Riverside. Contact us to confirm same-day availability for your address."
- Q: "Can I request an estimate before service?"
  - A: "Yes. We provide clear estimate guidance and explain recommended next steps before work begins. For urgent situations, call first so we can help you stabilize the issue and dispatch quickly."
- Q: "Are your plumbers licensed and insured?"
  - A: "CertPro works with licensed and insured plumbing professionals. We focus on safe, code-conscious work and clear communication so you know what to expect before and during service."

**Copy outline:**

**FAQ block (schema-ready draft):**

### `/contact` (Contact)
**Primary intent:** Minimize friction to start a service request.

**Trust + local requirements:**
- Keep urgency and reassurance balanced.
- Clearly state response expectation and what happens next.
- Include local service-area reminder near form/phone options.

**Copy outline:**
1. **Contact hero**
   - Fast help headline with dual path (call or form).
2. **What to expect**
   - Response timing, triage process, and follow-up.
3. **Form guidance**
   - Keep instructions concise and clear.
4. **Direct call option**
   - For urgent needs and immediate support.
5. **Service area reminder**
   - Coverage list and confirmation prompt.
6. **FAQ block**
   - Response timing, required details, emergency triage.
7. **Submission reassurance**
   - Confirmation language and next-step expectations.

**FAQ block (schema-ready draft):**
- Q: "How quickly will someone respond after I contact CertPro?"
  - A: "We review incoming requests quickly and follow up with next-step guidance as soon as possible. Emergency plumbing calls are prioritized for rapid response and dispatch coordination."
- Q: "What details should I include in my request?"
  - A: "Share your name, phone number, location, and a short description of the plumbing issue. Helpful details let us triage urgency and prepare the right support for your service call."
- Q: "Should I call instead of using the form for urgent problems?"
  - A: "Yes. If you have an active leak, backup, or no-water emergency, calling is the fastest path to immediate support. Use the form for non-urgent requests or when callback is preferred."
- Q: "Do you serve my city?"
  - A: "CertPro serves North Kansas City and nearby metro communities, including Liberty, Gladstone, Parkville, Smithville, Platte City, Kearney, and Riverside. Contact us to confirm local availability."

## Service Page Outline Template (Apply to All 12 Slugs)

### Required section structure
1. Service-specific hero (problem + fast outcome)
2. Symptoms/problems this service solves
3. Service approach and deliverables
4. Why CertPro for this service (trust proof)
5. Local availability statement
6. FAQ block (schema-ready)
7. Final CTA with reassurance

### Local intent insertion points
- Hero subcopy: include "local" and coverage-language naturally.
- Mid-page trust block: mention neighborhood/city service familiarity.
- Final CTA: restate area coverage + response expectation.

### Service-page CTA language pattern
- Primary: "Call Now - 24/7" or service-specific urgent variant.
- Secondary: "Request Service Estimate".

### FAQ schema pattern (minimum set per service page)
Each service page should include:
- "What are signs I need [service]?"
- "Do you offer emergency [service] in my area?"
- "How quickly can service be scheduled?"
- "What should I do before the plumber arrives?"
- "How does pricing/estimate guidance work?"
